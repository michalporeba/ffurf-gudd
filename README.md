# Ffurf Gudd (Hidden Form)

Exploration of ephemeral code, data, and algorithms as art. 

**Ffurf Gudd** is an exploration of the beauty of computer science and information architecture. 
It is an attempt to surface the otherwise hidden fabric of the most recent human experience. 

It seeks to transition the ephemeral—code, data, and algorithms—into tangible fine art.

## Core Pillars

### The Artifact
Historical code elevated to visual monuments. By freezing significant logic into physical form, we acknowledge its impact on our culture.

### The Mechanics
Visualising fundamental algorithms and their logic. Transitioning the abstract movement of sorting, searching, and computing into rhythmic patterns.

### The Metamorphosis
The transformation of information. Capturing the state-change as data shifts between human-readable text and its numeric, binary, or compressed skeletons.

### The Snapshot
Mapping the internal memory, state, and flow of machines. Visualising the cartography of RAM, stack growth, and the orchestration of parallel threads.
